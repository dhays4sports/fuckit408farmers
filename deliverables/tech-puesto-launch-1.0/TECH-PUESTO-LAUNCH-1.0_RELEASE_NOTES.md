# TECH-PUESTO-LAUNCH-1.0

Forward-only launch release for the Puesto technology-professional campaign.

- Replaces the `/tech/` all-at-once form with role tap, housing tap, then the minimum fallback checkpoint.
- Keeps first name, mobile, and explicit agency contact permission as the only required early identity fields.
- Keeps automated marketing SMS permission optional, separate, unchecked, versioned, and timestamped.
- Preserves same-origin `/api/lead`, durable CoverageFit delivery, direct Formspree fallback, retry, and anonymous continuation.
- Carries the bounded technology role and homeowner/renter track through the secure CoverageFit POST bootstrap.
- Adds explicit browser callback choices: exact time, when available, or no contact now.
- Preserves the existing Google Calendar confirmation and polished add-to-calendar page.

No underwriting, eligibility, discount, SMS-suppression, bind, life-vault, or producer-authorization boundary is changed.
