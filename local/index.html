Clear by design

Local perks stay separate from insurance.

408FARMERS Local is a community discovery program. Merchant offers, when available, are provided by participating businesses and are subject to each merchant's terms and availability.

  • No insurance purchase required.You do not need to buy a policy to access a public Local offer.
  • No quote required.You do not need to request an insurance quote to view or use a merchant perk.
  • No effect on insurance pricing or eligibility.Local participation does not change insurance rates, discounts, coverage, eligibility or underwriting.

The pilot is growing carefully. Merchants choose their own offer and remain responsible for their own terms.
